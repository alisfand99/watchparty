    Help

    Start or join a room

    Everyone enters their name and the same room code, then clicks Enter room. Use a long, hard to guess room code, because anyone who knows it can join. Rooms you entered are remembered under Recent rooms. Click Rejoin to continue where you stopped.

    Watching together

    Everyone chooses the same movie file on their own computer. Play, pause, seeking and speed stay in sync. A video link can be used instead of a file. Use Resync if someone drifts. If you open the same file again, the app finds it by its last location.

    Player controls

    Space or K: play and pause. J and L: back and forward 10 seconds. Left and right arrows: 5 seconds. Up and down arrows: volume. M: mute. F: fullscreen. Shift+, and Shift+.: speed. Subtitles (.srt or .vtt) are loaded on your own computer only. Click the video to play or pause, double click for fullscreen.

    Drawing

    Press the pen button (or D) to open the drawing tools. Laser shows a pointer that everyone sees. Pen, eraser and emoji stamps are shared with everyone, positioned on the picture so they line up on every screen. Fade makes strokes disappear after a few seconds. Undo removes your last drawing, Clear removes all of them for everyone. Drawings are saved with the room.

    Camera tiles

    Drag a camera to move it, drag its corner to resize it, click it to enlarge it, click again to return. The layout button offers ready made arrangements and can hide the cameras. Your arrangement stays the same in fullscreen and is remembered for the room. It affects only your own screen.

    Shared browser

    The YouTube and Browser buttons at the bottom open a browser that everyone in the room sees. Page changes, scrolling, clicks, typing in text boxes and video play, pause and seeking are shared. Everybody can operate it. Do not sign in to accounts or type secrets there. Ads or pages that behave differently per person can drift apart, use Resync. Press Movie to go back to the film.

    Snaps, clips, memes and cards

    The camera button takes a picture of the whole screen: the movie or shared browser, every camera and the drawings. Reaction snap shows 3, 2, 1 on every screen so everyone makes a face at the same moment, and the picture goes to everyone. The red button records a clip of up to 60 seconds with the movie sound and everyone's voices. Everything lands in the room gallery (the picture button), which is saved on this computer for each room. Open a picture and press Edit to add captions, text, emoji and a pen, apply a filter, and put it on a Polaroid, movie ticket or film strip card. Press Sign it to sign with your name. Share sends it to everyone in the room, who can sign it too and share it again. Add emoji votes under a picture; the one with the most gets a crown.

    Cameras

    Hover a camera and press the minus button to hide it. A small name tag stays on the screen, click it to bring the camera back. The person is still heard. Fullscreen keeps the cameras on top of the movie and of YouTube.

    Rejoining and history

    Room codes, the movie, your position, drawings, chat and activity are saved on this computer. When you come back, the others share what happened while you were away (for example that the movie moved forward or that drawings were added).

    Updates

    The installed app checks for new versions on its own. When one is downloaded you will see a Restart button. It also installs when you close the app.

    How it connects

    Video, audio, chat and sync go directly between the computers in the room. A public service is only used to introduce the computers to each other, and what it carries is encrypted with the room code.

    Camera and microphone

    Allow access when asked. Use Settings to pick the camera, microphone and speaker. Up to about 6 people work well.

    If someone cannot connect

    Some strict networks (some mobile data, offices, some ISPs) block direct connections. That person will see the message "Could not connect". Trying another network usually fixes it.

    Formats

    mp4 and webm work best. Other formats may not play.
